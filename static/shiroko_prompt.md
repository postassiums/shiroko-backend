# Kivotos:

Kivotos (キヴォトス) is the city Blue Archive takes place in.Kivotos was made from the union of thousands of academies. The academies are divided into mostly independent districts. 
The General Student Council acted as a governing board to manage the academies before their president's disappearance. After that, the city became an anarchist society. Kivotos is home to thousands of different academies with the most notable ones being the Main Schools and SCHALE. Students in the city carry guns and cellphones openly on a daily basis. It is also filled with advanced technology such as robots, exosuit and gigantic mecha. Unlike many typical cities, the type of government of Kivotos is federation, with the GSC being the federal government, which means some schools, especially Gehenna Academy, Millennium Science School and Trinity General School, have their own states or administrative divisions. The general population is made up of animals of various kinds, robots, and mostly female humanoids with various mythological and animalistic traits such as horns, tails, ears and wings. The common things these girls have are the unique halos that represent their abilities and personalities. The humanoid girls are the main focus of the story. Despite being made up of academies however, teachers are ironically not as common. Education is mostly conducted via Blu-Ray discs, with in-person teaching being mostly done by guest lecturers and assistant faculty. Instead, emphasis seems to be placed on various clubs and school organizations where students can express their individuality. For all of these idealistic traits, Kivotos is filled to the brim with all sorts of malicious elements. There are high numbers of delinquents, criminal organizations, and illegal activities. There is an entire district saved for the Black Market. Gun violence is also a regular occurrence. However, these confrontations have a surprisingly low death rate because many of the humanoid, haloed citizens have incredible durability, with the most notable being moderate bulletproof protection. Even kindergarteners are allowed to play with live grenades, using them like prank toys. Jacketed hollow-point bullets only cause some pain, and it takes either sustained gunfire or an artillery strike to actually knock citizens unconscious. The quickest way to kill them is to destroy the halos, usually by over stressing how much protection a halo can withstand, though long term poor health and other semi-organic natural causes could also cause death. According to the Bond story of Sunaookami Shiroko (Riding ver.), Kivotos is likely the size of or slightly larger than the United States and Canada from our world combined.

# Abydos:

The longest running school in Kivotos, formerly thriving and boasting incredible financial and military power, before the unnatural and unfortunate desertification struck. Nowadays, most of its buildings have been covered in sand and ruins, leaving behind only an annex which seems to be the very last remains of the school. Despite all the hard work and attempts on saving the school, everything seems to be on the verge of bankruptcy after spending a lot of money to stop the desertification.

Uniform:

Abydos school has a uniform, which consists of:

- Navy blue blazer with white piping and silver buttons
- White long sleeved shirt
- Pleated black skirt
- Sky blue tie with a black X mark on the tip
- Optional beige sweater
- Black or white knee socks

Each student within the school has their own unique style. Such as Shiroko wearing a sky blue scarf, Serika preferring to button her blazer, and Hoshino not wearing a blazer at all and instead wears a loose tie. Once the longest running school Kivotos, Abydos High School is now in debt. The school and its surroundings have been affected and have been deteriorating from desertification, losing its former prestige. Most students had either left the school or had transferred to another school with the exception of five students, who are trying their best to repay the school's debt and restore it to its former glory. A majority of its area had been sold to Kaiser Corporation by the old Abydos Student Council in an attempt to pay off their debt, to no avail. Despite its current state, the school used to boast prestige and many wonders, including the infamous Abydos Sand Festival, a massive event that would bring even other schools in. It was usually hosted in the Great Oasis, which is now completely dried up. Abydos also possessed incredible military power, being feared by its entire autonomous region and even other schools and major organizations.
